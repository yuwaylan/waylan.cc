# Portfolio redesign notes

The homepage is arranged as a visual sequence: a dithered cloud opening, a white resume and biography section, a continuous pink project field, a white archive and education section, and a black closing footer. The header remains a light set of navigation tabs, while the footer holds a low-motion wireframe torus and an accessible pause control.

The cloud banner is the original asset at `public/images/portfolio-clouds-v2.png`. It was generated with Codex built-in ImageGen for a stylized concept asset, then used as a raster background without editing.

Prompt used:

> Asset type: original panoramic website background image for a personal engineering portfolio. Create a wide 3:1 landscape of spectacular billowing cumulus clouds rendered as finely textured retro computer ordered-dither / newspaper halftone pixel art. Deep fuchsia, warm vivid pink, pale lavender and purple in the clouds, powder-blue sky visible in a large irregular opening across the upper center. Clouds frame the top and sides, swirl diagonally, occupy the upper two-thirds, and become very pale pink wisps before fading into pure white across the entire bottom quarter so this banner flows into a white webpage. Rich cloud volume with fine sharply defined dot texture at all scales, beautiful atmospheric depth, generous light in the center. Art direction: experimental 1990s computer graphics meets sophisticated contemporary editorial design; bold but airy. The result is only sky/cloud artwork, not a website mockup. No text, no letters, no logos, no interface, no frames, no objects, no silhouettes, no watermark. Wide panoramic composition approximately 3:1, at least 1800px wide.
